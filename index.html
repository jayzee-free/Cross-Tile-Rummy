Your rack

Cross Tile Rummy

Rummikub‑style tiles on a crossword grid. Empty your rack first by laying down runs and groups.

Players

How to play
  • Run: 3+ consecutive numbers in one colour (4‑5‑6 blue).
  • Group: 3 or 4 of one number, all different colours.
  • Play any valid set of 3+ tiles from your very first turn, and add to or rearrange sets already on the table whenever you like.
  • At the end of every turn you draw one tile, whether you played or not — unless you just emptied your rack and won.
  • Sets go across or down, crossword style. A tile can belong to an across set and a down set at once.
  • When you end your turn, every line of two or more touching tiles, across and down, must be a valid set. Runs can count either way: 7‑8‑9 or 9‑8‑7, across or down.
  • Wilds (the smiley tiles) stand in for any tile. You may play one ambiguously; once other tiles pin down its number or colour, it keeps that identity until it's replaced. Where two sets cross, it must be the same tile both ways. The small number on a wild shows what it stands for.
  • Your opening play can't use a wild from your hand. You can't go out on your first turn if you started it holding a wild.
  • To take a wild off the table, put the exact tile it stands for in its square. The wild must go straight back into a set with at least 2 tiles from your hand, and you declare exactly what it is.
  • You can go out on a wild.
  • Undo costs 3 tiles. If you can't fix the table yourself, Undo puts everything back where it was, you draw 3 tiles, and your turn ends. Taking back tiles you placed this turn with To rack is free.
  • Can't play? Just draw.
  • Pinch to zoom the table. It shrinks itself as play spreads out; tap Fit to see everything.
  • Pass the phone: set seats to Person for 2–4 people on one phone (computers can fill the rest). Between turns the tiles are hidden until the next person taps to reveal theirs.
  • Tap tiles to select them, choose Across → or Down ↓, then tap an empty square. Squares with tiles already on them are skipped, so you can lay tiles on both sides of a table tile.
  • Tiles you placed this turn have a green underline and can go back to your rack. Problem tiles get a red outline.

Declare your wild

You replaced a wild, so it must be declared as one exact tile now. It keeps that identity until someone replaces it.
